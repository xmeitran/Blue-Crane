About BCL

Blue Crane Logistics Co., Ltd.

Blue Crane Logistics Co., Ltd. (Công ty TNHH Tiếp Vận Sếu Xanh – BCL) is a logistics company focused on Vietnam – Cambodia cross-border logistics. We support shippers and freight forwarders with export, import and transit customs procedures, as well as container and truck transport through land border gates.

Guided by our slogan “Fly High, Delivery Fast”, BCL brings transport, customs clearance, documentation and delivery together in one clear process. Every request is reviewed shipment by shipment, so that the solution and quotation match your cargo, schedule and actual operating conditions.

  • One contact for customs clearance and transport
  • Hands-on experience at Vietnam – Cambodia border gates
  • Transparent quotations, confirmed per shipment

How we work

From request to delivery in four steps

  1. Send your request

    Cargo, route, quantities and documents via the online form, hotline or Zalo.

  2. Review & quotation

    We check HS codes, documents and route conditions, then send a clear quotation.

  3. Clearance & transport

    Customs declaration, border procedures and transport are coordinated by one team.

  4. Delivery & documents

    Cargo is delivered and the full document set is handed over to you.
