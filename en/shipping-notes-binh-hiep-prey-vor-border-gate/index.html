Preparation Notes for Shipping via the Bình Hiệp – Prey Vor Border Gate

18/11/2026

Practical notes on documents, vehicles and coordination on both sides when shipping cargo through the Bình Hiệp – Prey Vor border gate.

The Bình Hiệp border gate is in Tây Ninh province (part of Long An province before the 2025 administrative reorganization), opposite the Prey Vor gate in Svay Rieng province, Cambodia. It is the gate BCL focuses on most on the Vietnam – Cambodia corridor. Below are some preparation notes to help your shipment cross more smoothly.

1. Confirm in Advance That the Goods Can Be Cleared

Each border gate may have its own conditions on cargo types, inspection methods and accepted vehicles. Before stuffing, shippers should send the cargo description and expected HS code so that clearance through Bình Hiệp – Prey Vor can be checked, especially for goods subject to quarantine or specialized inspection.

2. Prepare Consistent Documents for Both Sides

  • Contract, commercial invoice and packing list with matching information.
  • C/O if the Cambodian importer wishes to claim preferential tariffs.
  • Licences, quarantine certificates or specialized inspection results where the goods require them.
  • Full details of the consignee and the customs agent on the Cambodian side.

Send scanned documents in advance so that the party handling procedures can check them, rather than discovering discrepancies only when the truck reaches the gate.

3. Choose a Suitable Vehicle

On the Bình Hiệp – Prey Vor route, BCL arranges 20DC and 40HC containers, trucks, flatbed trailers and side-wall trailers. The choice depends on the cargo type, packaging, package dimensions and handling requirements at pick-up and delivery. For oversized or heavy cargo, provide the dimensions and weight of each package so that lashing and weight limits can be checked.

4. Coordinate with the Consignee in Cambodia

Import procedures at Prey Vor are handled by the consignee or its agent, or coordinated by BCL where this is within the agreed scope. Both parties should agree in advance on who is responsible for the Cambodian procedures, the contact person and the final delivery location.

5. Plan Realistic Timing

Clearance time can vary with the channel assigned, physical inspection, quarantine requirements and conditions at the gate at any given time. Shippers should build a time buffer into the delivery plan, particularly for goods with a short shelf life or tight production schedules.

6. Packaging and Marking

  • Use sturdy packaging suited to road transport and repeated handling.
  • Mark shipping marks and package numbers clearly, matching the packing list.
  • For agricultural products, pay attention to moisture, ventilation and quarantine requirements.

Conclusion

Well-prepared documents, the right vehicle and good coordination on both sides are key to a smooth crossing at Bình Hiệp – Prey Vor. Specific conditions are confirmed by BCL for each shipment. Send a quote request with your cargo details for advice before you stuff the cargo.
