Xa Mát – Trapeang Phlong Border Gate

Road route through the Xa Mát – Trapeang Phlong border gate, linking Tây Ninh with Tbong Khmum, by container, truck, flatbed and side-wall trailer.

Route Overview

The Xa Mát (Tây Ninh) – Trapeang Phlong (Tbong Khmum) gate pair links Tây Ninh province with Tbong Khmum province in Cambodia. BCL arranges transport and customs procedures through this gate when it suits the customer’s pick-up point, delivery point and cargo type.

Typical Cargo

Agricultural products, bagged goods, materials, machinery and equipment and packaged cargo, subject to commodity policies and inspection requirements at the gate.

Vehicles

  • 20DC container (20 ft dry)
  • 40HC container (40 ft high cube)
  • Truck
  • Flatbed trailer
  • Side-wall (fence) trailer
